A deposit is required to reserve your appointment and is applied toward your service total. It is refundable if My Drip Nurse or the assigned provider cannot provide the appointment, including a provider no-show, or if you cancel at least 24 hours before the scheduled start time. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the appointment and patient no-shows forfeit the deposit. The remaining balance is collected at the appointment. Review the full policy: https://policy.mydripnurse.com
